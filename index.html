About

I am a Computer Science & Engineering graduate from Daffodil International University whose work sits at the intersection of machine learning, medical image analysis, and computational biology. My research is driven by a simple question: how can learning systems be made both accurate and trustworthy enough to support decisions in health?

Across my projects I have compared classical and deep-learning approaches under rigorous evaluation — grouped and nested cross-validation, external validation on independent cohorts, and explainability methods such as Grad-CAM — and carried models through to working web applications. I am particularly interested in methods that remain reliable under distribution shift and that integrate heterogeneous biological evidence.

Research interests

  • Explainable AI for Medical Imaging
  • Computational Biology & Gene Prioritization
  • Transcriptomic & Multi-source Evidence Integration
  • Transfer Learning & Generalization
  • Audio & Signal Classification
  • TinyML & Low-cost Health Sensing

T2D Gene Ranking

An Exploratory Machine-Learning Ranking of Type 2 Diabetes-Associated Pancreatic β-Cell Genes Using Heterogeneous Gene-Level Evidence

Lead author

  • Integrated 14 genetic, transcriptomic, tissue-expression, network, and pathway predictors to prioritize T2D-associated pancreatic β-cell gene candidates.
  • Ranked 3,868 genes; evaluated L2-regularized logistic regression with grouped five-fold cross-validation, where only 2.5% of labeled genes are positive — so precision-based scores are read against that low base rate.
  • Identified held-out candidate genes for further biological investigation.

GlaucomaDetect

Comparative Evaluation of Machine Learning and Transfer-Learning-Based Deep Learning Models for Explainable Glaucoma Detection from Retinal Fundus Images

Lead author

  • Compared SVM, XGBoost, EfficientNetB3, and DenseNet121 on 6,000 retinal fundus images.
  • Engineered HOG, LBP, and color-histogram features; applied two-stage transfer learning.
  • External validation on the independent VEIRC (India) cohort without retraining.
  • Applied Grad-CAM explainability and built GlaucomaDetect, a Flask web application.

UrbanSoundAI

Comparative Study of Optimized ANN and SVM Models for Environmental Sound Classification with Practical Web-Based Deployment

Lead author

  • Compared optimized Artificial Neural Network and Support Vector Machine models on UrbanSound8K.
  • Extracted 80-dimensional acoustic features from 40 MFCC coefficients (means and standard deviations).
  • Tuned with KerasTuner and GridSearchCV; developed UrbanSoundAI, a Flask app with waveform, Mel-spectrogram, and MFCC visualizations.
